## Full description (English)

**RaptiCKT — Resonance Automation Circuit Simulator**
Design, simulate and understand electronics — in real time, right on your phone.

RaptiCKT is a powerful, beautifully designed circuit simulator for electrical & electronic engineering students, hobbyists, teachers and researchers. Build analog, digital and mixed-signal circuits on an unlimited canvas, press Run, and watch voltages glow and currents flow.

⚡ BUILD & SIMULATE
• Unlimited workspace with pinch-zoom, smart wire routing and snap-to-grid
• 330+ components in 20 categories: sources, passives, semiconductors, analog ICs, digital logic, sensors, switches, outputs and meters
• Live voltage colouring, animated current flow and capacitor charge
• Tap switches and push buttons while the circuit runs; turn the rotary knob to change any value live
• Undo/redo, multi-select, copy, rotate, flip

🔬 TEST & MEASURE
• 6-channel oscilloscope with auto-scaling, frequency / RMS / peak-to-peak readouts, FFT spectrum and XY mode
• Digital multimeter with draggable probes: volts (DC & true RMS), amps, ohms and watts
• AC small-signal analysis with Bode plots, automatic −3 dB, resonance, bandwidth and Q detection
• DC sweep for I–V curves and transfer characteristics
• Operating-point tables and CSV export

🎓 RESEARCH-GRADE ENGINE
• SPICE-class Modified Nodal Analysis with Newton–Raphson and junction limiting
• Trapezoidal and backward-Euler integration, DC operating point with gmin & source stepping
• Real device models: Shockley diodes & Zeners, Ebers–Moll BJTs with Early effect (2N3904, 2N2222, BC547…), level-1 MOSFETs (2N7000, IRF540N, IRLZ44N…), JFETs, op-amp macromodels with GBW & slew rate (LM741, LM358, TL071…), coupled-inductor transformers, DC motors
• Export SPICE netlists to cross-check in ngspice or LTspice

🌡️ SENSORS INCLUDED
LDR, NTC thermistor, Pt100 RTD, type-K thermocouple, LM35, photodiode, phototransistor, solar panel, Hall sensor, strain gauge, force sensor, flex sensor, gas sensor, humidity sensor, microphone and PIR motion sensor — each with a live "environment" knob.

📚 LEARN WITH EXAMPLES
LED circuits, voltage dividers, RC/RL/LRC transients, filters and resonance, rectifiers and regulators, transistor amplifiers, MOSFET motor control, op-amps, 555 timers, logic gates, counters and 7-segment displays, sensor projects and more — each with an explanation.

💾 SAVE & SHARE
Save unlimited circuits on your device, export/import .rckt files, share circuits and export PNG images.

Made by RAPTI — Resonance Automation Professional Training Institute, Dhaka. Since 2019 we have been training skilled engineers for the automation industry: Industrial Automation (PLC), Electrical Control, General Electrical, House Wiring, Computer and IT courses. Learn more at rapti.net.
